TO CELEBRATE DANNY HILLIS'S 70TH BIRTHDAY

Patents you can play

Three of Danny's patents, read all the way down to the claims and then built into things you can actually operate. He has rather a lot of patents. It seemed a shame that so few of them were playable.

Pick a machine. Push its buttons. Nobody from the patent office is watching.

US 9,134,205 B2 · 2015
Dust Off

System and method for removing surface particles from an object

The airport puffer booth. A sensor picks a spot on a person, a jet knocks particles loose, and capture jets herd them into a collector for analysis. We replaced the air jets with lasers, because it is a birthday. Tap the subject, sweep the loose particles down into the intake, and try not to let the red ones drift off. The clock in the corner is the ten thousand year one.

US 7,857,767 B2 · 2010
Lumen

Lumen traveling device

A self propelling microrobot with wall gripping wheels, a sensor for a condition of interest, and an active portion that does something about it. You are the motion control circuitry. Drive through a branching vessel bed lit only by your own lamp, chase a sensor bearing that fibs when you are far away, dig the wheels in against the current, and burn the plaque out.

PLAY
US 9,480,991 B2 · 2016
Separator

Radiofrequency particle separator

A radiofrequency field heats mineral grains until the water around them boils, and the bubble carries the grain up to a skimmer. Frequency sets the skin depth, and it wants to land at about a tenth of the grain, so a big grain tunes low and a fine one tunes high. Ride the dial across the feed. The rock has no band of its own, and lifts whenever its size happens to match yours, which it will, at the worst moment.

THE ACTUAL PATENTS

US 9,134,205 B2, system and method for removing surface particles from an object. Hillis, Hyde, Kare, Ishikawa, Wood. Elwha LLC, granted 15 September 2015.

US 7,857,767 B2, lumen traveling device. Ferren, Hillis, Hyde, Ishikawa, Jung, Leuthardt, Myhrvold, Sweeney, Tegreene, Wood, Wood. Gearbox LLC, granted 28 December 2010.

US 9,480,991 B2, radiofrequency particle separator. Baym, Briggs, Gilbert, Hillis, Hyde, Ishikawa, Kare, Myhrvold, Myhrvold, Pan, Tegreene, Whitmer, Wood, Wood. Elwha LLC, granted 1 November 2016.

These are games, not implementations. We read the claims, borrowed the arrangement of parts, and then did whatever was more fun. Dust Off swaps the fluid jets for lasers, which the specification nowhere authorises and we are not sorry about. Separator's frequencies are invented numbers, although the relation they obey, skin depth falling with the square root of frequency so a bigger grain tunes lower, is the real one off the page. Nothing here simulates the physics. If you are planning to build a mining plant from this website, that is between you and your investors.

They run on a phone, and they are happier still on a laptop with a trackpad.

Happy birthday, Danny.
